What Is an AI Words Checker?

    
An AI words checker scans your text for the words and phrases that AI writing tools use far more often than people do. Think delve, a testament to, seamless and in today's fast paced world. It highlights each one, explains what to use instead, and lets you swap many of them for plain English in one click.

    
These patterns are measurable. A study of more than 15 million PubMed abstracts by Kobak and colleagues, published in Science Advances, found a sudden jump in style words such as delves, showcasing and underscores after ChatGPT arrived, and estimated that at least 13.5% of 2024 abstracts were processed with AI. GPTZero found phrases like today's fast paced world appear over 100 times more often in AI text than in human writing.

Is This an AI Detector?

    
No, and that is on purpose. People use these words too, and a flag never proves a text was written by AI. Treat the results as editing suggestions. Google says in its guidance on generative AI content that using AI is fine, as long as the content is accurate, original and made to help people rather than to game rankings.

Frequently Asked Questions

    
1What words does ChatGPT use too much?
Common ones include delve, testament, tapestry, underscore, pivotal, seamless, robust, leverage, vibrant and elevate, plus phrases such as in today's fast paced world, plays a crucial role and it's important to note. Research on millions of scientific abstracts and AI text samples shows these words became far more common after ChatGPT launched.

    
2Can this tool tell if text was written by AI?
No. It is a style checker, not a detector. People use these words too, so a flag only means a phrase sounds like AI writing. No tool can prove who wrote a text, and AI detectors often make mistakes on human writing.

    
3Does Google penalize AI written content?
Not for using AI. Google says appropriate use of AI is not against its guidelines. What matters is that the content is helpful, accurate and original. Content made mainly to manipulate rankings is treated as spam, however it was written.

    
4Is my text uploaded anywhere?
No. The checker runs entirely in your browser. Your text and word list are never sent to us, to an AI model or to anyone else, so it is safe for client work and unpublished drafts.
