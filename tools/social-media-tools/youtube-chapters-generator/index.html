What Is a YouTube Chapters Generator?

    
YouTube chapters split a video into named sections. Viewers see them as segments on the progress bar and as a list under the video, so they can jump straight to the part they want. Chapters also give Google clear labels for each part of your video, which can help it show key moments in search.

    
To add chapters manually, you type a list of timestamps and titles into the video description. The list has to follow strict rules, and a single mistake means YouTube ignores all of it. This generator cleans up rough notes and checks every rule from YouTube's chapters help page before you paste.

Tips for Chapters Viewers Use

    
    
      
  • Write titles people search for. "Adding timestamps in YouTube Studio" helps more than "Part 3".
    • 
      
  • Keep titles short. On phones only a few words show on the progress bar.
    • 
      
  • Do not overdo it. Five to ten chapters suit most videos. Very short chapters make the bar hard to use.
    • 
      
  • Saving your own list overrides automatic chapters. If YouTube already added chapters you like, you can leave them.

Frequently Asked Questions

    
1Why are my YouTube chapters not showing?
Usually one rule is broken: the first timestamp is not 0:00, there are fewer than three timestamps, they are out of order, or a chapter is shorter than 10 seconds. Channels with active strikes also cannot use chapters. Paste your list here to find the problem.

    
2How do I add chapters to a YouTube video?
Open the video in YouTube Studio, go to Details, and paste a list of timestamps and titles into the description, starting with 0:00. Save the video and the chapters appear within a few minutes.

    
3Do I need hours in timestamps for long videos?
Only for times past one hour. Use minutes and seconds like 12:30 below an hour and hours, minutes and seconds like 1:05:00 after it. The generator formats each time for you.

    
4Is my data sent anywhere?
No. The generator runs entirely in your browser. Your notes and video link are never uploaded or stored.
