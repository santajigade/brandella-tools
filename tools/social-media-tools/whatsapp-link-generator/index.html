What Is a WhatsApp Link Generator?

    
WhatsApp's click to chat feature lets anyone start a chat with you without saving your number first. You share a short wa.me link, and when someone taps it, WhatsApp opens a chat with you on their phone or computer, often with a message already typed. It is one of the simplest ways for a shop, clinic, coach or service business to turn website visitors and followers into conversations.

    
This generator builds the link exactly as described in WhatsApp's own click to chat guide: the full number in international format, without zeros, brackets or dashes, and the message encoded so spaces, emoji and line breaks work.

Frequently Asked Questions

    
1How do I create a WhatsApp link with my number?
Use https://wa.me/ followed by your full number in international format, with no plus sign, spaces, brackets, dashes or leading zero. For an Indian number 98765 43210 the link is https://wa.me/919876543210. This tool builds it for you.

    
2Can I add a message to a WhatsApp link?
Yes. Add ?text= followed by your message, encoded for web addresses. The message appears in the chat box when the link opens, and the person can edit it before sending.

    
3Does the link work for WhatsApp Business numbers?
Yes. wa.me links work the same way for personal and WhatsApp Business numbers, on phones and on WhatsApp for desktop and web.

    
4Is my phone number stored?
No. The link is built entirely in your browser. Your number and message are never uploaded or saved.
