What Is a Bold Text Generator?

LinkedIn, Instagram, X and Facebook posts are plain text, with no bold or italic button. A bold text generator gets around this by swapping normal letters for look alike characters from the Unicode Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols block. They look bold or italic, and because they are real characters, they stay styled wherever you paste them.

This tool works like a mini editor. Write your post as usual, wrap words in double stars for bold or single stars for italic, and copy the finished post in one click.

How to Use the Bold Text Generator

  1. Type or paste your post. Line breaks are kept.
  2. Mark words to style. Use **words** for bold, *words* for italic and ***words*** for bold italic.
  3. Pick a font. Sans serif blends in with most apps. Serif looks more formal.
  4. Copy the formatted post, or copy a single style from the list for a headline or bio.
  5. Changed your mind? Paste styled text and press Remove All Styling to get plain letters back.

Tips for Using Styled Text Well

  • Style a few words, not whole posts. Screen readers may read styled letters one at a time or skip them, which shuts out some of your audience.
  • Never style hashtags or names you want found. Search treats styled letters as different characters, so a styled hashtag will not match.
  • Watch your X count. X counts each styled letter as 2 characters under its counting rules.
  • Keep accents plain. Letters like é have no styled version, so mixed words can look uneven.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How do I write in bold on LinkedIn?

LinkedIn posts have no bold button. Write your post here, put double stars around the words you want in bold, copy the formatted post and paste it into LinkedIn. The bold letters are Unicode characters, so they stay bold.

2Does bold text work on Instagram, X and Facebook?

Yes. The styled letters are standard Unicode characters that work in captions, bios, comments and posts on all major apps. A few very old phones may show empty boxes instead.

3Is styled text bad for accessibility?

It can be. Screen readers may spell out styled letters one by one or skip them entirely. Use styling for a few words of emphasis rather than whole sentences, and never for hashtags.

4Is my text saved?

No. Everything happens in your browser and nothing you type is uploaded or stored.
