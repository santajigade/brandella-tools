What Is a PPC Budget Calculator?

A PPC budget calculator works backwards from what you want to achieve. Instead of guessing a number, you start with a goal, such as 60 leads a month, and use your conversion rate and cost per click to find the budget that goal needs.

The logic is simple: clicks needed = conversions ÷ conversion rate, and budget = clicks × CPC. You can also flip it and see what a fixed budget will buy.

How to Use the PPC Budget Calculator

  1. Choose how to plan: from a conversion goal, a revenue goal or a monthly budget you already have.
  2. Enter your conversion rate and average CPC. Use your own account history. If you are new, use the Google Ads Keyword Planner for CPC estimates.
  3. Add CTR to see how many impressions you need, and value per conversion to see expected ROAS.
  4. Copy the daily budget into your campaign settings.
  5. Check the table to see how much a better or worse conversion rate changes the plan.

How Google Ads Spends Your Daily Budget

RuleWhat it means for you
Average daily budgetMonthly budget ÷ 30.4, the average number of days in a month
Daily overdeliveryOn some days spend can reach up to 2 times your daily budget
Monthly charging limitYou are not charged more than 30.4 times your average daily budget in a month
Changing budget mid monthThe limit is worked out again from the new budget for the rest of the month

These rules come from Google's help page on managing your spend in Google Ads.

Budget Planning Tips

  • Start small and learn. Your first month tells you your real CPC and conversion rate. Plug those back in and plan again.
  • Leave room for learning. Smart bidding needs enough conversions to work well, so a tiny budget split across many campaigns rarely performs.
  • Budget by goal, not by habit. Give the most money to the campaigns with the lowest CPA or highest ROAS.
  • Plan for seasons. CPCs often rise during festivals and sale periods, so add a buffer.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How do I calculate my Google Ads budget?

Divide the conversions you want by your conversion rate to get the clicks you need, then multiply by your average CPC. 60 conversions at a 3.5% conversion rate needs about 1,715 clicks, which at ₹25 per click is about ₹42,875 a month.

2How do I turn a monthly budget into a daily budget?

Divide the monthly budget by 30.4, the average number of days in a month that Google Ads uses. A ₹1,00,000 monthly budget is a daily budget of about ₹3,289.

3Can Google Ads spend more than my daily budget?

Yes. On some days Google can spend up to 2 times your average daily budget, but over a month you will not be charged more than 30.4 times that daily budget.

4Is my budget data stored?

No. The PPC budget calculator runs fully in your browser and nothing you type is uploaded.
