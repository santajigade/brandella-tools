What Is CPA?

CPA means cost per acquisition, also called cost per action or cost per conversion. It is the average amount you pay in ads to get one result, such as a sale, a lead form, a sign up or a call.

The formula is CPA = ad spend ÷ conversions. Google Ads defines average CPA the same way: total cost divided by the number of conversions. Spend ₹75,000 for 150 leads and your CPA is ₹500.

How to Use the CPA Calculator

  1. Enter your ad spend for a period, such as last month.
  2. Enter conversions for the same period. Count only the action you care about.
  3. Add clicks if you also want your conversion rate and average CPC.
  4. Fill in the max CPA section with the value of one conversion, your margin and, for leads, how many become paying customers.
  5. Compare the two numbers. If your CPA is under your max CPA, the campaign makes money.

Tips to Lower Your CPA

  • Fix the landing page first. Doubling your conversion rate halves your CPA without touching bids.
  • Cut wasted spend. Search terms that never convert push CPA up. Add them as negatives.
  • Set CPA goals per conversion type. A newsletter sign up and a purchase should never share one target.
  • Be careful with Target CPA bidding. Start your target near your recent CPA and move it in small steps, as Google suggests in its Target CPA guide.
  • Lead businesses must track quality. A cheap lead that never buys is worth nothing, so use the close rate field honestly.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How do you calculate CPA?

Divide your total ad spend by the number of conversions. ₹75,000 spent for 150 conversions gives a CPA of ₹500.

2What is a good CPA?

A good CPA is one below your maximum affordable CPA, which is the profit you make from one conversion. For leads, multiply the value of a customer by your margin and by the share of leads that become customers.

3Is CPA the same as cost per conversion?

Yes, in practice. Google Ads labels it Cost / conv. Some people use cost per acquisition only for new customers, so check how your team defines a conversion.

4Does this CPA calculator store my numbers?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent or saved.
