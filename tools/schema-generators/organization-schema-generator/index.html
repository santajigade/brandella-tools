What Is Organization Schema?

    
Organization schema is structured data that describes your company or brand: its official name, logo, website, contact details and the profiles that belong to it. Google can use it in knowledge panels and to pick the logo shown for your site.

    
It also helps search engines and AI assistants connect your brand to your social profiles, which supports entity understanding.

Frequently Asked Questions

    
1Which page should have organization schema?
Your home page or a single page about your company, such as the About page. Google says it does not need to be on every page of your site.

    
2What is sameAs in organization schema?
sameAs is a list of URLs for your profiles on other websites, such as LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube or Wikipedia. It helps search engines confirm those profiles belong to you.

    
3What size should my logo be?
At least 112 x 112 pixels, in a format Google Images supports such as PNG, JPG or WebP. Check that it still looks good on a plain white background.

    
4Is my company data sent to a server?
No. This organization schema generator works fully in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded.
