What Is Video Schema?

    
Video schema is structured data that describes a video on your page using the VideoObject type. It gives Google the title, thumbnail, upload date, length and where to find the video file or player.

    
With it, your page can appear in video results and the Videos tab, and with chapters added, Google can show key moments that let people jump straight to a section.

Frequently Asked Questions

    
1Do I need video schema for embedded YouTube videos?
It is not strictly needed, since Google can often detect embeds, but markup makes the title, thumbnail, date and chapters explicit and can help your own page appear for the video.

    
2What is the difference between contentUrl and embedUrl?
contentUrl points to the video file itself, such as an MP4. embedUrl points to the player, usually the src of an iframe. Google prefers contentUrl when you can provide it.

    
3How do I write video duration in ISO 8601?
Use PT, then hours with H, minutes with M and seconds with S. For example, 5 minutes 42 seconds is PT5M42S. This tool converts it for you.

    
4Is my video information uploaded?
No. The video schema generator works only in your browser. Even the YouTube helper just reads the video ID from the link you paste, without contacting YouTube.
