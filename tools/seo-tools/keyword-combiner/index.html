What Is a Keyword Combiner?

A keyword combiner, sometimes called a keyword mixer, takes two or three lists of words and joins them into every possible combination.

Instead of typing out "buy running shoes", "best running shoes", "cheap trail shoes" and dozens more by hand, you enter each part once and get the full list instantly.

How to Use This Keyword Combiner

  1. List 1: modifiers such as buy, best, cheap, or review.
  2. List 2: your core products or services.
  3. List 3 (optional): locations or qualifiers like online, near me, or a city name.
  4. Choose match types if you are building a Google Ads campaign, then copy or download the list.

Duplicates are removed automatically, and the counter shows exactly how many keywords you will get.

Using It for Google Ads

Google Ads uses three keyword match types:

  • Broad match (no symbols) reaches the widest range of related searches.
  • Phrase match ("in quotes") shows ads for searches that include the meaning of your phrase.
  • Exact match ([in brackets]) targets searches with the same meaning as the keyword.

Tick the match types you need and paste the output straight into Google Ads Editor or the keyword planner. Pair it with a solid list of Google Ads negative keywords so the extra coverage does not waste budget.

Using It for SEO Research

Combined keywords are not a final keyword list. They are seeds. Paste them into a keyword research tool to check search volume and difficulty, then keep only the terms people actually search for.

Our guide to search volume vs keyword difficulty explains how to choose between them. When your list gets messy, clean it with the duplicate line and keyword cleaner.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How many keyword combinations can I create?

There is no fixed limit. The keyword combiner runs in your browser, so it comfortably handles thousands of combinations. Very large lists may take a moment to display.

2What is the difference between broad, phrase and exact match?

Broad match reaches the widest range of related searches, phrase match requires the meaning of your phrase, and exact match targets searches with the same meaning as the keyword.

3Should I use every combination in my campaign?

No. Use the list as a starting point, then check search volume and remove combinations nobody searches for or that do not fit your offer.

4Is this keyword combiner free?

Yes. It is completely free, needs no signup, and nothing you enter is uploaded.
