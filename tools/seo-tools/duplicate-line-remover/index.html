Why Clean Your Keyword Lists?

Keyword lists grow messy fast. Exports from different tools overlap, the same phrase appears with different capitals, and stray spaces make identical keywords look different.

A clean list saves time in research, avoids duplicate keywords in Google Ads, and gives you accurate counts when planning content.

How to Use This Duplicate Line Remover

  1. Paste your list with one item per line. It works for keywords, URLs, emails, product names or any other list.
  2. Choose your options. By default it ignores case, trims spaces and removes blank lines.
  3. Sort if needed, alphabetically or by length.
  4. Copy or download the clean list.

The counters show how many lines went in, how many came out, and how many were removed.

What Each Option Does

  • Ignore case treats "SEO Tools" and "seo tools" as the same item and keeps the first one.
  • Trim and collapse spaces removes spaces at the start and end, and turns double spaces into single ones.
  • Remove blank lines deletes empty rows.
  • Convert to lowercase makes the whole list consistent, which suits keyword research and URL lists.
  • Sort orders the list A to Z, Z to A, or by length, which is handy for spotting long tail keywords.

Where It Helps

Use it after merging exports from several keyword tools, before uploading keywords or negative keywords to Google Ads, or when combining URL lists for a content audit.

It pairs well with our keyword combiner. For strategy, read Google Ads negative keywords and content audits explained.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How do I remove duplicate keywords from a list?

Paste the list with one keyword per line. The tool removes duplicates instantly, and with 'ignore case' on, it also catches the same keyword written with different capitals.

2Which duplicate is kept?

The first time an item appears is kept, and later repeats are removed. Your original order stays the same unless you choose a sort option.

3Is there a limit on list size?

No fixed limit. Because the tool runs in your browser, it handles tens of thousands of lines comfortably on most computers.

4Is my list uploaded?

No. The duplicate line remover works entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
