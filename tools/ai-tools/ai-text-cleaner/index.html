What Does the AI Text Cleaner Do?

    
Text copied from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other AI tools often carries baggage you cannot see. Zero width characters, no break spaces and direction marks hide between words. Markdown stars and hashes show up as clutter when you paste into WordPress, Word or an email. And every answer seems to open with "Certainly!" and close with "I hope this helps".

    
This AI text cleaner strips all of that in one pass and tells you exactly what it changed. It also lists every hidden character it found, so you can see what was really in the text. Everything runs in your browser.

Tips for Publishing AI Assisted Text

    
    
      
  • Cleaning is not rewriting. It fixes characters and formatting. Add your own facts, examples and voice so the page is genuinely useful.
    • 
      
  • Removing hidden characters does not hide AI use. They are not a reliable watermark, and AI detectors look at wording, not invisible characters.
    • 
      
  • Want headings and lists in WordPress? Turn off the Markdown option and convert the text with the Markdown to HTML converter instead.
    • 
      
  • Read it once more after replacing dashes. A comma is right most of the time, but sometimes a full stop reads better.

Frequently Asked Questions

    
1Does ChatGPT add hidden watermarks to text?
Some AI answers contain invisible characters such as no break spaces or zero width spaces, but they are not a reliable watermark. They usually come from formatting. Removing them makes your text cleaner, but it does not change how AI detectors judge the wording.

    
2Will it break Hindi or emoji text?
No. The cleaner keeps zero width joiners and non joiners next to Indian script letters and inside emoji sequences, because those are needed to display the text correctly.

    
3Why replace long dashes?
Heavy use of long dashes has become a common sign of AI written text, and many style guides prefer commas or full stops. You can switch the option to keep them if you like them.

    
4Can I keep the headings and bullet points?
Yes. Untick the Markdown option to keep the symbols, or paste the text into the Markdown to HTML converter to turn headings and lists into real HTML for WordPress.
