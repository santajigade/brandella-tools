What Does the AI Crawler Log Analyzer Do?

Analytics tools like GA4 cannot see AI crawlers, because bots do not run JavaScript. The only place their visits show up is your server access log. This tool reads those log lines and pulls out every known AI bot, from OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Meta, Amazon, Apple, ByteDance and others.

For each bot you see who runs it, why it visits, how many requests it made, how many different pages it read and how many errors it hit. It also counts real people who arrived from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Claude, so you can compare bot interest with actual clicks. Your log never leaves your browser.

How to Use It

  1. Download your access log. In cPanel or hPanel look for Raw Access or Access Logs. On a VPS it is usually in /var/log/nginx/ or /var/log/apache2/.
  2. Paste the lines into the box. A day or a week of traffic works well. The common combined log format is read line by line.
  3. Read the bot table to see which companies crawl you and for what purpose.
  4. Check the errors column. Bots hitting 404 or 500 pages waste their visit on content that does not exist.
  5. Download the CSV to keep a monthly record and spot trends.

Tips for Reading Your Results

  • Search bots matter most for visibility. If you want to be cited in ChatGPT or Perplexity answers, make sure their search bots can reach your key pages.
  • Watch for crawl waste. Bots stuck on tag archives, pagination or login pages are a sign to tidy internal links or block those paths.
  • Verify big spenders. Anyone can fake a user agent. Check heavy hitters against the official IP lists before you trust or block them.
  • Compare bots with clicks. Many bot hits but few AI referral visits usually means your pages are read but rarely cited.

Frequently Asked Questions

1Why can't I see AI crawlers in Google Analytics?

AI crawlers request your HTML but do not run JavaScript, so tracking tags like GA4 never fire. Server access logs record every request, which is why log analysis is the reliable way to measure them.

2Should I block GPTBot and ClaudeBot?

It depends on your goals. GPTBot and ClaudeBot collect content for model training. Blocking them does not stop OAI SearchBot or Claude SearchBot, which power AI search results. Many publishers block training bots and allow search bots.

3Which log formats does the tool read?

It reads the combined log format used by Apache, Nginx, LiteSpeed and most hosts. For other formats it still finds the bot name, path and status code in each line, as long as the user agent is included.

4Is it safe to paste my server logs here?

Yes. The analysis runs entirely in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Logs can include visitor IP addresses, so avoid sharing the raw file elsewhere.
